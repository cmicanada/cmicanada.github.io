Statement of Faith

Statement of Faith

We believe:

1. The Bible

The Old and New Testaments are inspired by God and record His complete revelation of salvation for humanity. They are the highest authority for Christian faith and life.

2. God

God is self-existent and eternal: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, eternally one God in three persons. He creates, sustains, and governs the universe and all things.

3. Christ

Jesus Christ is the only Son of God, fully divine and fully human. Sent by the Father, conceived by the Holy Spirit, and born of the virgin Mary, He was crucified, bodily resurrected, and ascended to sit at the right hand of the Most High. He is God’s anointed prophet, priest, and king. At the last day, Christ will personally return to judge the living and the dead.

4. The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is equal in honour and glory with the Father and the Son. He exalts Christ, guides us into all truth, and convicts the world concerning sin, righteousness, and judgment. He sanctifies people and empowers them to be witnesses of Jesus Christ.

5. Humanity

Human beings were made in God’s image but fell into sin through rebellion and need God’s salvation. Through regeneration by the Holy Spirit and justification by faith in Jesus Christ, they are reconciled to God. All believers are priests, called to equip the saints, fulfil their ministries, and build up the body of Christ.

6. Redemption

The blood, substitutionary death, and resurrection of Jesus Christ are the sole foundation of our justification and redemption. Only those who believe in and receive Jesus Christ are born anew through the Holy Spirit and become children of God.

7. The Church

The church is the body of Christ, with Christ as its head, uniting all believers born anew through the Holy Spirit. Through worship, discipleship, fellowship, service, and witness, it fulfils the Great Commission.

8. Missions

Our God is a missionary God. The Holy Spirit mobilizes the whole church, based on the whole Bible, to proclaim the whole gospel to the ends of the earth until the Lord returns.

9. We believe the Bible clearly teaches that marriage, established by God, is the union of one man and one woman.
