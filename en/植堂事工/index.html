Field Missions

CMI Canada currently has two missionaries, 盛行楚 and 李愛珍, who serve as a married couple in Cambodia. Their dedicated service for the Lord has borne abundant fruit. Working with other missionaries and local workers to advance the gospel, they lead or assist the following ministries:

  • Dormitory ministry: three dormitories focus on discipleship, welcoming university students and sharing the gospel with them. Two local pastors supported by CMI Canada also lead students in outreach elsewhere.

  • Bilaga Youth Gospel Centre: a place for current students and alumni to worship and fellowship, and a church established through the dormitory ministry.

  • Holy Mountain Church: a remote rural church in Pursat Province, established through repeated evangelistic outreach by dormitory ministry workers under the leadership of CMI missionaries. A missionary couple from Canada serves there alongside local workers. CMI Canada is developing a worship and education centre at Holy Mountain, combining missions, poverty relief, and education to serve impoverished villages.

  • Children’s education: Chinese, English, and Bible story classes at the CMI office in Phnom Penh and nearby villages share the gospel with local children.

  • The two missionaries also serve as Cambodia field directors, receive short-term and visiting mission teams, care for local workers, and undertake poverty relief and other work.
