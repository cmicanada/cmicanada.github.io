Care Ministries International
Care Ministries International

Care Ministries International (CMI) was founded by Pastor 李秀全. It first registered with the Hong Kong government in 1997 under the name Care Society, then formally established operations in the United States in 2001. It is an interdenominational Chinese-speaking faith mission agency focused on cross-cultural missions.

  • Mission Vision

“The gospel to China; the gospel from China” is CMI’s vision. Through ministry into China, we move toward sending the gospel out from China. Through full-time and professional service, based on the whole Bible, we mobilize the whole church to bring the whole gospel to the whole world.

  • Core Values

Care expresses Christ’s love for us and our love for China. The four letters of CARE convey CMI’s ministry priorities:

     C: CHRIST CENTEREDNESS — Christ at the centre.

     A: ATONEMENT — redemption as the way.

     R: RECONCILIATION — reconciliation as the purpose.

     E: EVANGELIZATION — missions as the calling.

  • Mission Strategies

In response to the needs of the field, CMI sends long-term, professional, tentmaking, short-term, and associate missionaries to serve through compassion, medical care, education, evangelism, and training, bearing witness to the gospel and nurturing communities of Christ’s disciples.

  • Mission Fields

Since its founding, CMI has sent missionaries from Taiwan, the United States, Canada, New Zealand, and restricted-access regions to serve cross-culturally in Thailand, Cambodia, and restricted-access fields. It also supports gospel work among ethnic minority groups in Myanmar. In recent years, it has actively explored ministry in Japan and other countries.

  • Sending Bases

CMI has established regional offices in the United States, Taiwan, New Zealand, Australia, Canada, Japan, and restricted-access regions. These serve as bases for recruiting, training, and sending missionaries.

  • Main Ministries

  1. Mission mobilization: mobilizing, equipping, and calling disciples to become missionaries. Through different approaches, they pioneer ministries, share the gospel, establish churches, and train disciples of Jesus Christ in the field to fulfil the Great Commission.

  2. Mission prayer: guided by “missions and prayer” and “prayer is the root of missions; missions are the fruit of prayer,” CMI calls partners to pray for missions, establishes monthly prayer fellowships, and holds annual mission prayer conferences in the western and southeastern United States and Taiwan.

  3. Missionary care: establishing missionary care fellowships and encouraging care throughout a missionary’s service, from recruitment to retirement.

  4. Mission education: in partnership with seminaries, CMI offers six-to-eight-week summer Mission Field Training (MFT), emphasizing cross-cultural practice and training in the field. Partners have included Logos Evangelical Seminary in the United States and Taiwan’s China Evangelical Seminary, Lutheran Theological Seminary, Disciple Training Theological Seminary, Central Taiwan Theological Seminary, Holy Light Theological Seminary, Truth Theological Seminary, Alliance Theological Seminary, Baptist Theological Seminary, and a seminary in a restricted-access region. A hundred participants have completed MFT, with numbers increasing each year. Several now serve in mission fields; others have returned home after two years of short-term service.

  5. Medical outreach: medical professionals visit Cambodia each year, serving physical needs as a way to minister to spiritual needs.

  6. Rapha Institute: developing a nursing institute in Cambodia to train local Christian nursing professionals and help the gospel take root among the people of Cambodia.

  7. Chinese-language teaching: sending missionaries to teach Chinese at universities or secondary schools while sharing the gospel, leading people to Christ, and training disciples of Jesus Christ.

  • Mission Funding

CMI is a faith mission agency. Its funding comes through God’s grace, expressed in the support and giving of brothers, sisters, and churches.
