ABOUT CMI CANADA

From Canada,
responding to the Great Commission through care.

CMI Canada is an interdenominational Chinese-speaking mission agency founded by Chinese Christians and focused on cross-cultural missions.

We seek to be a bridge between supporting churches and mission fields, bringing love to people in need.
