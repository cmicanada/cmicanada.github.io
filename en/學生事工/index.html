Field Support

As a sending base, CMI Canada works closely with mission fields to develop and advance the work there:

Financial support:

  •  Living expenses and ministry needs of our two current missionaries.

  •  Support for the Bilaga Youth Gospel Centre and dormitory ministry. CMI Canada provides living expenses for two local preachers so they can focus on serving students. The dormitories are almost free for students from poor families, so supporting churches also help meet operating costs.

  •  Holy Mountain Church: the financial needs of local workers and the worship and education centre under construction.

  • Financial support for local preachers, enabling them to devote themselves to serving the Lord.

Support through prayer and care:

  • We regularly share prayer needs from the field so everyone can pray for them. We also hold a monthly prayer meeting and welcome you to join us.

  • We maintain contact with missionaries and local workers in the field and attend to their needs.
